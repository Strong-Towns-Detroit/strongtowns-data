# Data and evidence licensing

The MIT License applies to software in this repository. It does not relicense
third-party source material, municipal records, maps, imagery, or generated
datasets.

Git contains schemas, reviewed research fixtures, content hashes, snapshot
manifests, promoted pointers, and provenance metadata needed to audit builds.
Raw downloads and generated payloads are excluded and must be obtained from
their named providers or the separately controlled project archive under the
provider's terms.

Before adding an evidence file, record its source and retrieval date and
confirm that redistribution is permitted. If those rights are unclear, retain
only its identity, hash, and provenance record.
